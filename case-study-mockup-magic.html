Case study

Mockup Magic

An AI-assisted product mockup and advertising platform designed to shorten the path from finished apparel artwork to marketplace-ready content.

Generative AINext.jsSupabaseTypeScriptKonvaE-commerce UX

The problem

For independent apparel brands and print shops, finishing artwork is only the beginning. Sellers still need realistic mockups, lifestyle imagery, marketplace variations, social creative, and organized exports—often across several tools and services.

User pain points

  • Professional photography, models, and locations can be expensive.
  • Flat overlays often fail to follow fabric folds, texture, and lighting.
  • Sellers switch among mockup, editing, AI, social, and export tools.
  • Every additional production step delays launch.
  • Small teams handle design, store operations, content, and advertising themselves.

The solution

I designed Mockup Magic as a consolidated workflow with three product areas: a Mockup Studio for garment compositing and print-finish controls, an AI Model Studio for fictional lifestyle models with commercial controls and guardrails, and an Ad & Export Center for marketplace and social outputs.

ArtworkProduct mockupAI lifestyle modelAd creativeMarketplace export

The central UX idea

The product follows one continuous journey: upload artwork, create the product mockup, generate a lifestyle presentation, create advertising assets, and export the final content. The user should not need to decide which unrelated app to open next.

Realistic mockups

The Mockup Studio is designed around texture blending, color changes, garment compositing, and print-finish presets so artwork feels integrated with the product rather than placed on top of it.

What this demonstrates

Generative-AI product design, e-commerce workflow thinking, UX/UI, prompt-driven feature definition, image-generation orchestration, and designing around a user’s entire commercial workflow rather than one feature.
