About me

Hi, I’m Alfonzo.

I’ve spent more than half my life in the technical world, but I’ve been an artist for as long as I can remember. I love solving problems, creating things, and figuring out how ideas can become something real and useful.

AI feels like a new creative medium to me — a way to take an idea that exists in my head and shape it into a working product, a visual experience, an automated system, or something that did not exist before.

Over time, I found a natural harmony between technical troubleshooting, AI, empathy, and creativity. Those parts of me work together. I can understand the problem, think about the person experiencing it, explore the possibilities, and then build toward a solution.

I live and breathe the creative process. For me, the best part is not just the finished result — it’s the process of discovering what something could become.
