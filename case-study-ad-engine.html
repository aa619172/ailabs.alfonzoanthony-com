The problem

Small businesses and solo operators often build ads, log campaign data, capture leads, and follow up across several disconnected tools. Repeated setup creates friction, inconsistent messaging, and delayed follow-up.

User pain points

  • Campaign setup is repetitive and time-consuming.
  • AI copy still requires manual handoffs into campaign tools.
  • Lead information can become scattered.
  • Slow follow-up can cause prospects to go cold.
  • Small teams often lack dedicated ad operations support.

The solution

I built a multi-step n8n engine that treats the prompt as one component inside a larger operating system. Product and campaign inputs become ad content, campaign actions, logged data, webhook-driven lead handling, and timed follow-up.

Campaign inputAI ad generationMeta campaign actionsLoggingLead captureFollow-up

Prompt engineering as systems design

The prompt had to generate useful advertising language while respecting downstream structure. I designed for tone, product context, campaign objectives, reusable formatting, and consistency so later workflow steps could consume the output reliably.

Measured outcome

I live-tested this specific engine with one Meta awareness campaign. It reached 16,653 people on $26.53 in ad spend, with a displayed cost of $1.59 per 1,000 people reached.

What this demonstrates

Prompt engineering, workflow orchestration, data logging, lead automation, debugging, and the ability to move an AI concept into a real business process with measurable output.
